# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place for constructive technical collaboration.

Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or publishing another person's private information. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's contact channel or GitHub's reporting tools. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
